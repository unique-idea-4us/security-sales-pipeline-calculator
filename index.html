    How the math works

    Most owners set a revenue goal and then guess at how much selling it takes. This calculator starts at the end of the funnel and divides backward by each stage's rate, so every number above is what the next stage needs.

    Proposals = contracts / close rate
    Walkthroughs = proposals / walkthrough to proposal rate
    Appointments booked = walkthroughs / show rate
    Conversations = appointments / booking rate
    Calls = conversations / reach rate

    Because each stage divides the one after it, a small change early in the funnel moves everything behind it. Try raising the booking rate by a few points and watch the call count drop.

    Where to look first when the numbers look too big

    Reach rate

    Low reach usually means a bad list. Calling the front desk instead of the property or facility manager burns most of your dials. Fix the list before you add callers.

    Booking rate

    Ask for a short site walkthrough, not a meeting about your company. Open with a reason to call today, like a new project, a new manager or a renewal date.

    Show rate

    Confirm the day before, send the address and the name of who is coming, and book walkthroughs within a week of the call while interest is still fresh.

    Close rate

    Proposals built from a real walkthrough win more often than quotes sent from a phone call. Scope what you saw and price it clearly.

    Questions

    Where do I get my own rates?
    Count the last three to six months in your CRM or spreadsheet: calls made, conversations with decision makers, appointments booked, walkthroughs held, proposals sent and contracts signed. Divide each stage by the one before it.
    What if I don't track calls yet?
    Start with the stages you do know, like proposals and signed contracts, and track calls for the next month. Even rough numbers beat guessing.
    Should short-term contracts count?
    Yes. Set the contract length to the typical term, so a one-month event job and a one-year building contract are valued correctly.
    Why is the headline number walkthroughs, not calls?
    Walkthroughs are where security contracts are won. Once you know how many you need, the rest of the funnel is what it takes to fill your calendar with them.
